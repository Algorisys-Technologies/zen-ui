# 12.9.0

*2026-11-13*

**Components for the shop floor, in every binding.** This release adds what a
manufacturing screen needs and a general-purpose library does not have: a
machine's state, its OEE, the story of its shift, the plant's andon board, why
it stopped, what was scanned, what was measured, and the work instructions an
operator follows. They ship in React, Solid, vanilla and web components at
once, over one shared core, so a plant screen written in one binding reads the
same in another.

Nothing breaks. Everything here is a new component, a new prop or a new
token. One thing looks different, deliberately: outlines that were always
meant to draw now do (see *Fixed*).

## New: the shop floor

- **`StatePill`** says what a machine is doing — running, idle, stopped,
  fault, waiting, setup, cleaning, maintenance, no data — from one vocabulary
  that also knows how OEE counts each state. A starved machine and a faulted
  one share the "down" colour, because both are lost time even though one is
  quiet. Colours are tokens (`--zen-state-*`), so a plant that draws planned
  stops in violet can.
- **`OeeGauge`** shows OEE as a half-circle with Availability, Performance and
  Quality underneath. Give it the counts and it does the arithmetic
  (`oeeFromCounts`); give it nothing and it shows a dash, not a 0% that would
  read as the worst shift of the year.
- **`StateTimeline`** draws a machine's shift as a strip where every stretch is
  exactly as wide as it lasted. A gap in the data is hatched, not painted over
  with the last known state. Click a stretch to drill in.
- **`AndonTile` / `AndonBoard`** is the plant board you read from across the
  room. It sorts the machines that need someone to the front, and when the
  board itself stops updating it greys out and says how old it is — a frozen
  board otherwise looks exactly like a calm plant.
- **`ReasonCodePicker`** is a touch grid for why a machine stopped or a part
  was scrapped: big targets, categories, and search that matches the code, the
  label or the description, accents ignored.
- **`ScanInput`** is a field for a USB barcode scanner. It tells a scan from
  typing by the keystroke timing, can refuse a typed code where traceability
  needs the real label, and can catch scans made while no field has focus.
- **`ProductionProgress`** shows good and scrap against the target, with scrap
  drawn and counted separately so it is never mistaken for progress.
- **`MeasurementInput`** checks an inspection reading against its limits as it
  is typed — pass, near the limit, fail — with a scale built from the limits,
  and reads `10,05` as a number where the locale writes it that way.
- **`StepRunner`** walks an operator through work instructions one step at a
  time: enforced order, checklists that must all be ticked, readings that must
  pass, and an issue flag that stays on the step until it is dealt with.
- **`LiveIndicator`** says whether a live screen is current: "Updated 12s
  ago", turning to a warning when updates stop and to "Offline" when the
  connection drops.
- **`ShiftNavigator`** steps through shifts in the plant's own time zone. A
  night shift belongs to the day it starts, and the clocks changing does not
  move a shift.
- **`PlannedVsActual`** says "256 pcs behind plan" in words and draws the plan
  line under the output, with a tolerance so one piece does not flip the
  headline.
- **`RateBars`** draws output in equal time buckets, each bar coloured by what
  the machine was doing then, so a short bar explains itself.
- **`LineCard`** puts one line on a card: its state, the order it is running,
  good and scrap against target, OEE, and an ETA that only appears while the
  line is actually running.
- **`CapacityHeatmap`** is booked hours against available hours, resource by
  period, as a real table you can read with a screen reader, every cell a
  button for the drill-down.
- **`ColumnMapper`** maps a spreadsheet's columns onto your fields: matched by
  name and alias, never guessed from position, with every bad cell reported
  before anything is imported.
- **`GenealogyGraph`** traces a lot, order or unit both ways — what it was made
  from and what it went into — which is the question a recall starts with.
- **`RoutingGraph`** lays out a process's steps and which must finish before
  which. It refuses a link that would close a loop, and says why.

## New: planning

- **Drag work onto the schedule.** `ScheduleBacklog` lists the jobs not yet
  placed; drag one onto `ProductionSchedule` (or place it from the keyboard)
  and the chart hands you a proposal through `onPlace`. Like rescheduling, it
  proposes and never applies, so undo stays yours.
- **Late jobs show it.** Give an operation a `due` date and a bar that ends
  after it gets a red end cap and says "Late by 2 h" to a screen reader.
- **Pareto charts.** `AnalyticChart` has a `pareto` type: largest first, the
  running share of the total on its own axis, and the causes that together
  reach `threshold` (80% unless you say) in full colour — the ones to fix first.

## New: everything else

- **Touch density.** `data-density="touch"` on any element takes inputs,
  selects, buttons, table rows and menu items to 48px for tablets and gloved
  hands. It scopes to a subtree like a theme: one operator panel can be touch
  while the rest of the page is not.
- **Sidebar headings and a current-page mark.** `SidebarMenuSubLabel` heads a
  run of rows inside a group ("Transactions", "Pricing"), in the open list and
  in the collapsed rail's flyout. The line down an open group is now themeable,
  and can carry a mark beside the current page:

  ```css
  :root {
    --zen-sidebar-guide-current: var(--zen-color-primary); /* off unless set */
  }
  ```
- **Labelled radio groups.** `RadioGroup` takes `label` and `description`, and
  `RadioGroupItem` takes `label`, so a group no longer needs a hand-wired
  `<fieldset>`.
- **Icons for business areas** — `tag`, `cart`, `package`, `factory`,
  `briefcase`, `book`, `percent`, `building`, `award`, `shield`, `chart` — so
  an icon rail has a glyph per workspace, and `scan-barcode`.

## Fixed

- **Outlines that never drew now do — this is the visible change.**
  DataTable's selected row and focused cell, SpreadsheetGrid's selected cell
  and MediaTimeline's focus ring were all meant to be outlined and were not:
  the class that sets the outline's style was being dropped. If your screens
  have a DataTable with selection, the selected row now has its 1px primary
  outline.
- **Solid: the Sidebar leaked memory.** Every time a group closed, its list
  stayed in memory with a listener — and after the first close, some of
  Kobalte's own after-animation work stopped running for the rest of the
  session. An app that opens and closes groups as the user moves between pages
  grew without limit.
- **Collapsing the Sidebar closed groups your app controls.** If you keep
  your groups' open state (to open the group the current page is in),
  collapsing to the rail reported a close the user never made, and the group
  came back shut. `onOpenChange` now reports only what the user did.
- **Solid: `NativeSelect` showed its first option** whatever `value` said,
  when the options were rendered from a list.
- **Radio groups:** the selected dot rendered squashed in React, vanilla and
  web components; `BoundRadioGroup`'s label was not connected to the group;
  and `<zen-radio-group>` ignored its starting value.
- **Vanilla and web components: the Gantt, ProductionSchedule and
  PlanningCalendar view switchers announced no current view** to a screen
  reader.
- **Web components: `<zen-gantt>` and `<zen-production-schedule>` broke when
  their data attribute changed** after the first render. Setting the data as a
  property was never affected.
