# 13.2.0

*2026-11-18*

**Follow an order across weeks on one scrolling timeline.** Gantt and
ProductionSchedule can show the whole plan as a single timeline you scroll
sideways, with Day, Week and Month choosing how far you are zoomed in rather
than which single period you are looking at. A bar that runs past the end of a
week is never cut off.

Nothing breaks. It is off until you turn it on, and every chart without it
behaves exactly as before.

## New

- **One continuous timeline.** Turn on `continuous` and the chart covers the
  whole plan in one scrollable timeline:
  - **Day, Week, Month, Quarter and Year set the zoom.** Week shows a day per
    wide column, Month a day per narrow one, Day every hour. Switching zoom
    keeps you looking at the same dates.
  - **No bar is cut off** at the edge of a week or a month, so an order can be
    read from its first operation to its last.
  - **Previous and Next scroll by one period** from what is on screen, and
    **Today** scrolls to now. The title above the chart names the period in the
    middle of the screen as you scroll.
  - **The date row is on by default** in this mode (see 13.1.0's
    `headerGroups`), because a scrolled timeline needs the month or the date in
    view. Set `headerGroups={false}` to turn it off.

  ```tsx
  <Gantt tasks={plan} continuous defaultView="week" />
  <ProductionSchedule resources={cells} operations={jobs} continuous defaultView="day" />
  ```

  In web components it is an attribute:

  ```html
  <zen-gantt continuous default-view="week"></zen-gantt>
  ```

  It stays light on long plans: only the columns near the screen are drawn, so
  an hourly zoom across several weeks is as quick as a single day. On a very
  long plan at a fine zoom (more than 1500 columns), the timeline covers a long
  stretch around the current date rather than the entire plan.

  If you control the date yourself with `date` and `onDateChange`, nothing
  changes: Previous, Next and Today still report the new date, and the chart
  scrolls to it.

## Changed

- **Web components: leaving out `header-groups` now means "the default".**
  Before, an absent attribute meant "off". The default is still off, except with
  the new `continuous`, which turns the row on. A chart without `continuous`
  looks exactly as before.
