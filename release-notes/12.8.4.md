# 12.8.4

*2026-11-09*

**The Sidebar collapses and expands smoothly, in every binding.** Until this
release, only the sidebar's width animated. Everything inside it changed on the
first frame: each icon jumped about 100px sideways and slid back, the toggle
you had just clicked moved out from under the pointer, labels vanished at once
when collapsing and drew over your page when expanding, and group headings
snapped 24px.

You don't need to change any code; upgrade and the motion is smooth. A
collapsed rail ends up exactly where it did before, and an expanded sidebar at
rest lays out exactly as before.

## Fixed

- **Rows stay in place while the sidebar moves.** Icons ease to the centre of
  the rail and labels fade and truncate. The sidebar clips anything that no
  longer fits, so nothing spills over the page and no row changes height while
  the width is moving. Group headings fold away smoothly instead of jumping.
- **The toggle stays under the pointer.** The header no longer restacks when
  the sidebar collapses, so you can click the toggle again without chasing it.
- **The overlay sidebar slides in and out** instead of popping into view. In
  React it no longer slides shut when the page loads.
- **Nested menus (`SidebarMenuSub`) open and close smoothly**, and the row no
  longer jumps when the sidebar collapses. On the collapsed rail, clicking an
  item whose flyout is already open now closes the flyout. It used to close it
  and open it again.
- **Solid: a controlled `SidebarMenuSub` keeps its flyout open.** Opening it
  from your own state no longer closes it again straight away.
- **Reduced motion is respected.** The animation follows
  `--zen-duration-fast`, so it stops under `prefers-reduced-motion`. To retune
  it, set the new `--zen-sidebar-duration` and `--zen-sidebar-ease` tokens.

Thanks to Tanuv Nair for this fix.
