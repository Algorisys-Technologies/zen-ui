# 10.7.1

**The radius scale gains two steps.** `--zen-radius-xl` (12px) and
`--zen-radius-2xl` (16px) sit above `lg`, continuing the existing
4 → 6 → 8 progression.

Nothing was removed, renamed or restyled. Existing radii are untouched, so
this is additive for every consumer.

## Why

The scale stopped at 8px, which is smaller than most card and panel corners
want. Consumers reaching past `lg` had nowhere to go and hand-wrote the value.

Measured in SkillsEngine, which uses zen-ui across roughly 200 components:
eleven distinct radii in the codebase, and **every one above 8px was an
arbitrary value** — 10px, 12px, 20px and 1rem. None of them were themeable,
and no theme override could reach them. That is ~40 hand-written values that
now have a token.

## Using them

```html
<div class="zen-rounded-zen-xl">…</div>   <!-- 12px -->
<div class="zen-rounded-zen-2xl">…</div>  <!-- 16px -->
```

The tokens live in `@algorisys/zen-ui-core`, so every binding picks them up
without a per-binding change and parity is unaffected.
