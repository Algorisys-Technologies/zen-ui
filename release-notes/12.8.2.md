# 12.8.2

*2026-11-07*

**A form full of Solid date fields no longer holds on to memory after it
closes.** Each `DateInput` used to carry a whole calendar popover with it,
open or not. In an app that opens and closes many such forms (an ERP's
invoices, each with a posting date and a due date), a closed form could stay
in memory: about 7 MB at a time in the ERPNext port's leak check, where the
check failed four runs out of four.

Nothing is removed or renamed, and the field looks and types exactly as
before. Only Solid is affected.

## Fixed

- **Solid: `DateInput` mounts its calendar only while it is open.** The field
  itself is a plain box with its calendar button; the popover and the
  calendar are created when the button (or Alt+↓) opens them and disposed when
  they close. A form of date fields now lets go of everything when it closes.

## Changed

How the calendar opens and closes is the same, with one detail made explicit:

- **The calendar button toggles.** Press it again to close the calendar.
  Clicking inside the field while the calendar is open leaves it open, so you
  can still type; clicking anywhere else closes it.
- **Focus comes back to the field** after you pick a day or press Esc, as
  before. If you closed the calendar by clicking elsewhere, focus stays where
  you clicked.
