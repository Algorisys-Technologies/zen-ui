# 13.4.1

*2026-11-19*

**`barAccent` now marks Gantt milestones too.** In 13.4.0 a milestone ignored
`barAccent`, so a chart that coloured its bars by shift, line or customer left
its milestones unmarked. Now a milestone draws your accent colour as a border
around the edge of its diamond, and its status colour still fills the middle.

Nothing else changes. If you do not pass `barAccent`, milestones look exactly
as they did in 13.4.0.

## Fixed

- **Milestones ignored `barAccent`.** The colour you return now appears as a
  2px border inside the diamond. It sits inside rather than around the
  diamond, so dependency arrows still end exactly on the diamond's points.

  ```ts
  <Gantt
    tasks={plan}
    barAccent={(t) => t.customerFacing && "var(--zen-color-accent-purple)"}
  />
  ```

## Demo site

- The Gantt page's **Milestones** section now shows all three statuses,
  including a late milestone (its date has passed and it is not complete), and
  marks the customer-facing gates with an accent.
