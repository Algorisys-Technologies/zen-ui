# 12.1.1

Two fixes to `ObjectPageLayout`, in every binding. Nothing else changed, and a page
that used it correctly before looks the same.

## Jumping to a section scrolled the page around the layout

Clicking an anchor in the section bar scrolled the layout's content to that
section, as it should, and also scrolled every scrollable box around the layout:
the page, an app shell's panels, the demo's own content pane. An app whose shell
hides its overflow saw its header and navigation slide off screen with a gap
left underneath. A box with `overflow: hidden` hides the overflow but can still
be scrolled by script, and that is exactly what happened.

The jump now scrolls only the layout's own content scroller. It still lands the
section just under the sticky bar, smooth or instant as before, and the scroll-spy
behaves the same. Verified in all four demos: before, the surrounding page moved
on every jump; now it does not, and the section lands at the bar's edge.

If you had worked around this with `overflow: clip` on your shell, that is still
correct and can stay.

## The Solid binding wrote debug lines to your console

The Solid `ObjectPageLayout` shipped five `console.log("DBG …")` calls, which
printed on every scroll and section jump in your app (17 lines for one click in
the demo). They are gone.
